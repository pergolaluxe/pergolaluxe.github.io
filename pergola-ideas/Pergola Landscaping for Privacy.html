Pergola Landscaping for Privacy: 17 Stunning Ideas to Transform Your Outdoor Space

Published on April 4, 2025

When you think about creating your own backyard oasis, a pergola often becomes the centerpiece of that dream. But without the right touches, it can feel a little… exposed. Whether you're relaxing, entertaining, or simply enjoying a cup of coffee, privacy is key to truly enjoying your pergola. That’s where smart landscaping for privacy comes in.

Explore landscaping ideas to enhance privacy around your pergola, turning it into a serene and secluded sanctuary you’ll never want to leave.

Why Privacy Matters in Pergola Landscaping

Creating privacy around your pergola isn’t just about blocking the view from nosy neighbors. It’s about crafting an intimate space where you feel safe, comfortable, and connected with nature.

Assessing Your Pergola’s Location and Needs

Before planting or installing anything, take a good look at your pergola’s surroundings.

Sunlight, Wind, and Visibility Factors

  • Is it in full sun or part shade?
  • Are there high winds in your area?
  • How visible is it from the street or neighbor’s yard?

Orientation Tips for Privacy

  • Position open sides away from direct lines of sight.
  • Use surrounding trees or fences as anchors for privacy features.

Using Tall Hedges and Shrubs for Natural Barriers

Tall, dense plants are one of the oldest (and most beautiful) tricks in the book.

Best Evergreen Varieties

  • Arborvitae: Stays green all year and grows fast.
  • Boxwood: Neat and elegant.
  • Holly: Adds height and seasonal color.

Fast-Growing Options

  • Leyland Cypress and Privet are excellent if you want results fast.
  • Visit Petal and Spade for more gardening tips.

Climbing Plants and Vines for Vertical Privacy

Climbing plants on a pergola create a romantic, garden-like feel.

Top Vine Choices

  • Clematis: Colorful and lush.
  • Star Jasmine: Fragrant and fast-growing.
  • Ivy: Classic, hardy coverage.

Trellis and Pergola Integration

  • Add lattice panels or wire guides for vines to climb.
  • Encourage them to wrap around pergola beams.
